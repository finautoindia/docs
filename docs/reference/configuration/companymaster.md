🏢 Company Master
The Company Master is a key setup section used across all modules in the software.

📥 Tally Import Method
When data is imported from Tally, the Company Master is automatically created.

Ensure PAN is entered in Tally → F11: Features → Statutory & Taxation.

If PAN is missing, the Company Master will not be generated during import.

➕ Manual Creation Method
For non-Tally imports (e.g., Excel), you must create the Company Master manually.

Go to the Company Master section in the software.

Click the ➕ (plus sign) to add a new company.

Enter:

Company Name

PAN (mandatory)

Other details like CA, address or contact, Director DIN (optional)

Click SubmitSave to complete.

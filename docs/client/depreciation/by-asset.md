# Depreciation by asset

Drill into a single fixed asset to review capitalization, depreciation history, and disposal details.

## Prerequisites

- Asset exists in [Fixed asset register](register.md)

## Steps

### Step 1: Open by-asset view

1. Go to **Fixed Assets → By Asset**.
2. Search or filter for the asset code or name.

![Depreciation by asset detail view](../../assets/images/depreciation/by-asset-01.png)

### Step 2: Review asset lifecycle

1. Inspect capitalization date, purchase and sale values, and year-wise depreciation.
2. Cross-check against [Depreciation chart](chart.md) totals for the FY.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Resolution |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| Asset not found | Wrong company/FY | Switch company bar selection |
| History incomplete | Asset added mid-year | Verify capitalization date in register |

📘 Share Capital Import Process

This guide outlines the steps to import share capital details into the software using an Excel template.

<h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">🧭 Step 1: Navigate to the Share Tab</h3>
Open the client software.

Click on the "Share" tab from the main menu.

<h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">📥 Step 2: Download Excel Template</h3>
Go to Subscribed → Share Capital section.

Scroll to the bottom of the page.

Click on "Download Excel Template" to get the format for share capital data entry.

<h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">📊 Step 3: Import Share Data</h3>

Fill in the share details in the downloaded Excel file.

Navigate to Import → Import from Excel under the Share Capital tab.

Upload the completed file and follow the on-screen instructions to import.

<h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">🧾 Step 4: Update Holder Categories
After import, go to the Holder List.</h3>

Select the category for each shareholder (e.g., Promoter, Holding Company, etc.).

<h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">🔄 Step 5: Manage Changes in Future Years
In subsequent years, if new shareholders are added or existing ones retire:</h3>

Go to Tax Transfer Logs under the Share Capital tab.

Make necessary adjustments to reflect changes in shareholding.

## <h2 style="font-weight: bold; color: #0073e6;">🌟 Advanced Features</h2>

The **Advanced Usage** of Finauto builds upon the basic functionality by enabling additional core modules for a more complete financial reporting experience.

### ✅ What’s Included

In addition to everything covered under [**Basic Usage**](#basic-usage-of-the-software), the advanced setup includes:

- 🧾 **Fixed Assets Module**  
  Enables creation and management of a full **PPE Register**, along with automatic calculation of depreciation and plotting of the **PPE Notes** in the Balance Sheet.

- 💼 **Share Capital Module**  
  Allows importing and updating shareholder details, categorizing them (e.g. Promoter, Holding Company), and managing capital movements year-on-year through **Tax Transfer Logs**.

## <h2 style="font-weight: bold; color: #0073e6;">🚀 Basic Usage of the Software</h2>

Finauto is designed for quick and efficient financial report generation with minimal setup.

### <h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">📥 Step 1: Download Sample Data</h3>
Users can log in to their customer profile at  
🔗 [https://www.finautoindia.com/user/login](https://www.finautoindia.com/user/login)  
and download any of the following from the dashboard:

- 📁 Excel Sample Data (`Demo Data - Excel`)
- 🧾 Tally Sample Data (`Demo Data - Tally`)
- 🗜️ Zip Files containing prefilled trial balance and ledgers

These help in testing the software's functionalities without first entering detailed company setups.

---

### <h3 style="color: #e67e22; font-weight: bold;">📊 Step 2: Auto-Generated Financial Reports</h3>
Once data is imported, the following reports are automatically generated:

- 📄 **Balance Sheet**
- 📈 **Cash Flow Statement**
- 📉 **Profit & Loss Statement**
- 📌 **BS Notes** (Balance Sheet Notes)
- 📌 **PL Notes** (Profit & Loss Notes)
- 🧮 **Balance Sheet & P&L Groupings**

---

### ⚠️ **Not Included in Basic Usage**

This basic process **does not include**:
- Setting up the **PPE (Fixed Asset) Schedule** or related **PPE Notes**
- Entering **Shareholder details** under Share Capital

These advanced features can be configured later via their respective modules in the software.

---

💡 *Use this method to quickly test the software's reporting capabilities before going deeper into module-wise configurations.*

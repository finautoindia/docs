    CA Profiles

    The CA Profiles tab allows you to create and manage Chartered Accountant (CA) profiles used across the system.

    🧾 Purpose CA profiles are used to assign a practicing Chartered Accountant to companies. The assigned CA’s details will be printed in the Balance Sheet salutation when the statement is generated.

    🛠️ How to Create a CA Profile Go to Configuration → CA Profiles.

    Click on "Add New" or the ➕ button.

    Enter the required details:

    Name of the CA

    Firm Name

    Membership Number

    Address / Contact details (optional)

    Click Save.

    🔗 Linking with Company Master After a CA profile is created, go to Company Master.

    Select the company to which the CA is to be assigned.

    Choose the desired CA from the CA Profile dropdown.

    Save the changes.

    📄 Where It Appears The selected CA’s name and firm will appear at the bottom of the Balance Sheet, under the salutation or certification section.
